My card

When the facilitator calls an excuse that’s on your card, tap it to circle it. Five in a row, column or diagonal wins. The middle square is free.

Print cards

Prefer paper? Download a sheet of numbered cards, two per page, and open it in any browser to print. Every card number makes a different card, and the same number always makes the same card, so you can check winners on the spinner screen.

Running the game

  1. Put the “Spin the wheel” screen on the projector.
  2. Have people open this page’s “My card” on their phones, or hand out printed cards.
  3. Spin, read the excuse aloud in your best student voice, and pause so people can circle it.
  4. When someone calls bingo, check their card number under “Check a winner.”
  5. Plan on 15 to 20 spins and about 8 to 10 minutes for the first bingo. Bigger rooms finish a little faster.
  6. Play a second round for “blackout” or four corners if the room is having fun.

Questions for afterward

  • Which of these have you heard this semester? Which ones turned out to be true?
  • Behind the funny ones are real pressures: work schedules, caregiving, transportation, money. Which of these do our students face most?
  • What does your attendance or late-work policy ask a student to prove, and what does that cost them?
  • Would a no-questions-asked option, like one late pass or a dropped quiz, change what you hear?
