About the series

Who it's for

Faculty and staff in every discipline. No prior experience with AI tools is needed.

Self-paced materials

Each tutorial works on its own, so you can catch up on a session you missed or review later.

Use AI responsibly

Follow your institution's AI policy, and never upload student records or other private information to AI tools.
