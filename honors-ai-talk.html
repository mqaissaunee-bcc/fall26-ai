Your turn

Let's start one building right now

  • What's your major?
  • What's one thing in it that is hard to learn, practice, or keep track of?
  • Who would use a tool for that, and what would it show them?

We'll pick one idea, describe it to the AI in plain language, and let it work while we look at other tools.

Keep it small

One concept, something to do, a way to check yourself

  • NursingDosage calculation practice with worked answers
  • BusinessBreak-even calculator with a chart that moves as you change price and cost
  • PsychologyClassical or operant conditioning? Read a scenario and sort it
  • BiologyStep through the stages of mitosis, then put them in order
  • Criminal justiceIs this search legal? Fourth Amendment scenarios
  • HistoryTimeline of one event, with causes and effects to match
  • EducationSort sample exam questions by Bloom's taxonomy level

Build a single web page that helps a [major] student understand [concept]. Explain it in three short paragraphs for someone seeing it for the first time. Add one interactive part where the student can [change, sort, or step through something] and see the result. End with five practice questions that give feedback on each answer. Make it work on a phone and be accessible.

Too broad? Ask: what is the one thing in that course people get stuck on?
